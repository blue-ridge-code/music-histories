A modular library of standalone film-and-listening courses for adult learners.
**Cinematic Music History** is the documented initiative; **Course I** is the
first established course, and **Amadeus** is Chapter 1 and the first workbook
production pilot.

Courses combine roughly 10–15 core films, optional extensions, a curated YouTube
listening course, and a companion workbook or study guide. Film and music open
questions about culture, geography, technology, power, migration, identity, and
historical change. Listening can work **Before, Inside, Beside, After, or Bridge**.

Start with the [project index](docs/INDEX.md), [scope](docs/PROJECT.md),
[source inventory](sources/SOURCE_INDEX.md), and [agent rules](AGENTS.md).
The [local work plan](docs/work-plan.md) names deliverables, reviewers, and gates.

The first milestone is one proofed **Who’s Who** page with a traceable record
from research brief to final image. Page 11, **Mozart’s Hand**, and page 9,
**The Commendatore Scene**, then test the system before full-chapter production.
See the [Amadeus pilot](courses/course-i/amadeus/README.md).

**Current state:** planning active; source recovery and the first visual-identity
decision are pending. The owner reports a 14-page approved manuscript, six listening
selections, and v0.2 prototypes. These files have not yet been inspected here.
Future course titles and scope remain open.

on 2026-11-16. Private source locators and raw manuscripts, prompts, images, and
