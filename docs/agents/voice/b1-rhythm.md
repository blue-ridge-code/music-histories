# B1 · Rhythm

> Component of the [language persona system](../language.md) — the musical beat
> made by long and short sentences. Kickoff fills "Project setting" when voice
> matters; blank = template defaults.

## What it is

Rhythm is the sentence-level texture of prose: the beat produced by alternating
lengths, stress patterns, and pauses. Elegant style has "a noticeable flow or
rhythm" (Journalism U); "the pauses between the notes — ah, that is where the art
resides" (Schnabel, via Wheaton). Distinct from A4 pacing: rhythm is the
bar-to-bar feel of the sentences; pacing is the large-scale speed of the story or
argument.

## Levers

- Sentence-length mixing — the core lever (executed via A3 syntax).
- Pauses as material: commas, dashes, paragraph breaks.
- Sound: euphony, alliteration, stress patterns — used or deliberately avoided.
- The read-aloud test: prose that lurches fails, whatever the dials say.

## Template examples

### Combine a choppy series

**Before:** "Agents draft. Agents do not send. Agents do not spend. Agents do not
destroy. Agents ask."

**After:** "**Human gates** — agents draft but never send, spend, or destroy without
asking."

_Why: One strong stress (`Human gates`) leads into a balanced three-verb series. Source:
[`README.md`](../../../README.md)._

### Let the conclusion land

**Before:** "Registry choices should be revisited in response to the appearance of an
alternative that demonstrates improved quality or cost characteristics because the
system is designed to permit replacement."

**After:** "Re-evaluate picks when something better appears — swap-ability is the point."

_Why: The dash creates a pause before the short concluding phrase. Source:
[`README.md`](../../../README.md)._

## Kickoff questions

- Is read-aloud part of this project's review standard?
- Monotony tolerance: is an even, flat beat the goal (manuals, references) or a
  failure (features, essays)?

## Default (minimal kickoff)

Moderate variation, no strict monotony requirement, read-aloud not mandatory as a review
standard. Minimal kickoff writes this default into "Project setting" as-is; the full
A1–B3 workshop is what refines it later, and is where feature/essay work sets a real
monotony-avoidance standard.
