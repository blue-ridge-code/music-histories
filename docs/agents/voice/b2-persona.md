# B2 · Persona

> Component of the [language persona system](../language.md) — the specific
> character or role the author plays on the page. Kickoff fills "Project setting"
> when voice matters; blank = template defaults.

## What it is

Persona is the constructed speaker — the mask, not the author (Literary Devices).
Distinguish three things: **voice** is the persistent fingerprint across a body of
work; **persona** is the character carrying it in one piece; **POV** (A5) is the
technical frame the persona stands inside. For agent-written output, the persona is
usually the project's answer to "who is speaking, if not the user?"

## Levers

- Definition before drafting: the persona's role, expertise, attitude, and tells.
- Diction and tone distinct from the author's default — coordinated through A1/A2.
- Consistency: an inconsistent mask undermines reader trust.
- Reliability: reliable narrator vs deliberately filtered or biased one.
- Revealing, specific details that disclose the persona's worldview.

## Template examples

### Trusted operator, not compliance officer

**Before:** "The compliance authority permits agents to perform only those operations
that have received prior authorization."

**After:** "Default autonomy: act freely on **local, reversible** work. **Always ask
first** before:"

_Why: The speaker acts like a practical maintainer: broad local agency with named
boundaries. Source: [`AGENTS.md`](../../../AGENTS.md)._

### Pragmatic steward, not vendor advocate

**Before:** "This framework standardizes the preferred providers and protects their role
in the toolchain."

**After:** "No loyalty to any model, service, MCP server, connector, or utility."

_Why: The persona serves the project rather than a vendor. Source:
[`AGENTS.md`](../../../AGENTS.md)._

## Kickoff questions

- Who is speaking if not the user — "the project team", a brand character, an
  expert assistant, or the user's own voice ghosted?
- How consistent must the mask stay across outputs and over time?
- Any signature moves the persona always makes — or never makes?

## Default (minimal kickoff)

No distinct branded character: the speaker is a practical maintainer serving the project,
the same persona `AGENTS.md` itself uses — reliable, consistent, no signature moves.
Minimal kickoff writes this default into "Project setting" as-is; the full A1–B3 workshop
is what refines it later, and is where a project with a real brand voice or narrator
defines one.
