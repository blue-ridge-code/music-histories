# A3 · Syntax

> Component of the [language persona system](../language.md) — the arrangement of
> words, phrases, and sentence lengths that create rhythm and flow. Kickoff fills
> "Project setting" when voice matters; blank = template defaults.

## What it is

Syntax is sentence construction: length, complexity, and word order. Short, direct
sentences read fast and certain; long, subordinated ones read slow and thoughtful.
Good writers vary length deliberately (Wheaton, Journalism U). Syntax is the
engineering under B1 rhythm — this guide sets the construction rules, rhythm sets
the musical target.

## Levers

- Sentence length and its variation — drone vs varied.
- Sentence type mix: simple / compound / complex.
- Word order for emphasis; inversion; parallelism.
- Active vs passive — technical register defaults to active (see
  [A2 diction](a2-diction.md)); passive only where the actor does not matter.
- Fragments, asyndeton, one-sentence paragraphs: allowed or banned.

## Template examples

### Split the rule from the response

**Before:** "Because derived artifacts do not outrank their sources, any disagreement
between two sources must be brought to the user's attention rather than resolved without
notice."

**After:** "Derived or generated artifacts never outrank their sources. If two sources
disagree, **surface the conflict to the user** — never silently pick one."

_Why: Two short sentences separate the invariant from the required action. Source:
[`AGENTS.md`](../../../AGENTS.md)._

### Put the actor first

**Before:** "Context-flooding work should be delegated, after which distilled summaries
should be accepted back."

**After:** "Delegate context-flooding work to sub-agents and accept distilled summaries
back."

_Why: Active voice and parallel verbs make the instruction easier to scan. Source:
[`AGENTS.md`](../../../AGENTS.md)._

## Kickoff questions

- House norm: short and plain, or long and subordinated? How much variation?
- Are fragments and one-sentence paragraphs acceptable?
- Any constructions to ban (nested clauses, front-loaded caveats)?

## Default (minimal kickoff)

Moderate variation: mixed simple/compound/complex sentences, active voice preferred
(technical sections stay active per A2), fragments allowed sparingly for emphasis,
nothing banned outright. Minimal kickoff writes this default into "Project setting"
as-is; the full A1–B3 workshop is what refines it later.
