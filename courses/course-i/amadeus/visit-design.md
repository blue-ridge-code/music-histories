# Amadeus · three-page visit-design brief

Status: authoring proposal, not approved page copy. Reviewer: project owner.
Source: owner direction S16, manuscript S09 (pages 2, 9, 11), registry S04,
prototype evidence S11/S12, and [learning design](../../../docs/learning-design.md).
Exact private source files/locators remain in ignored raw storage.

## Shared approach

Target typical 5–15-minute workbook visits. A longer activity can span visits with
a useful stopping point and a small optional return cue; stopping is not failure.
Visits below are offered paths, not a required timetable or numbered levels.
Keep guidance available; no learner tests, rubrics, scores or progression gates.
All time estimates are authoring targets awaiting pilot evidence.

This brief does not edit the source manuscript, replace the exact puzzle/key,
invent an archival artifact, or add mandatory work. Newly proposed support,
sequencing and shortened copy need owner review before production.

## Page 2 · Who’s Who / Stage & Backstage

**Learning job:** connect names with social roles and a film moment; notice that
theatrical/social frames change how a person is understood (O1/O3).

The recovered draft already contains finding, placing and framing moves. They
need not all be completed in one sitting. Preserve the existing puzzle geometry,
letters and answer key, including source-to-key verification before final layout.

| Possible visit | One useful purpose | Natural pause and return |
|---|---|---|
| First, 5–15 minutes | Enter the theater setting and begin the existing name search; use the name list/support as needed | Stop anywhere in the search. A mark can show where to resume; no completion quota |
| Another visit, 5–15 minutes | Continue the same asset and connect encountered names with the source's role cues | Keep one chosen name/role as a small bookmark; consult explanatory notes without penalty |
| Optional later return, about 5 minutes | After viewing, bring back one person and reconsider how the film or theatrical frame shaped that impression | Return to a concrete moment if useful; no additional puzzle or written response is required |

**Scaffolding proposal:** let the theater cutaway establish the setting, keep
short instructions close to the exact puzzle, and offer one source-checked
role/context example outside it. Do not create new names, new answers or a second
role-matching test. Whether the page needs explicit pause markers is a layout
question, not a reason to add explanatory text automatically.

**Editorial work next:** verify the reusable puzzle and answer-key asset; resolve
the approved manuscript revision; compare the existing instructions with a shorter
candidate; inspect whether illustration can carry context without obscuring the
learner action. Keep all proposed exact-copy changes in the gated production record.

## Page 11 · Mozart’s Hand

**Learning job:** inspect a real artifact and consider what notation can preserve
and what performance supplies (O2/O3, supporting O4). This is not a notation exam.

| Possible visit | One useful purpose | Natural pause and return |
|---|---|---|
| First, 5–10 minutes | Look closely at the actual facsimile, with one modeled, visible feature if useful | Bookmark a detail or question without needing to finish an inventory |
| Another visit, 5–15 minutes | Return to the source and develop one thought about what the ink communicates or leaves open | Keep an optional short note or simply leave the page ready to revisit |
| Optional later connection | Compare a verified feature with a recording or the score-as-map activity when those assets are ready | Not required to move on; do not claim a performance is of the facsimile without verifying the work/source |

The current manuscript's feature prompts must be checked against the chosen
image. If a correction or other named feature is absent/illegible, change the
proposed prompt through review rather than fabricate a mark or misdescribe the
source. Keep the facsimile faithful; annotations belong outside it or in a clearly
separated overlay. No generated facsimile stands in for archival evidence.

**Production prerequisite:** obtain the real, inspectable scan, verify attribution
and reuse terms, and prove its legibility at placed size. The registry identifies
a candidate K.488 autograph; selection is not final source/rights verification.
Do not squeeze the artifact to fit more questions or exposition.

## Page 9 · The Commendatore Scene

**Learning job:** experience the assigned scene and its change of dramatic frame;
let the music carry the demand (O1/O4). The source manuscript already uses a
minimal before/after response and an optional further question.

- A short preparation/return can fit around five minutes of workbook use.
- The scene's listening time is separate; verify the exact recording's scene
  boundaries before giving timestamps or promising a total visit duration.
- Preserve the minimal response instead of adding a name drill, score worksheet,
  detailed recall list or required writing during listening.
- A later chapter can invite an optional memory of this scene when it serves a
  new connection; do not install another task on this deliberately quiet page.

**Production prerequisite:** exact assigned scene, adequate white space, legible
minimal copy, and the source-recorded composition-first visual direction. The
full-opera recording remains a container, not the assignment. This page is a
direct test of the Inverse Play Law, not the place to cover every course outcome.

## Internal review and handoff

The owner/editor checks the following as production questions, not learner rubrics:

- Does each offered visit have a clear action and a comfortable stopping point?
- Does resuming avoid repeating setup or losing a partly completed puzzle?
- Is recall invited before a reminder without withholding help or factual correction?
- Does each visual do a useful job, with exact puzzle/archive evidence intact?
- Are time estimates honest about separate viewing/listening and support needs?
- Does the quiet page remain quiet, and can exploratory work be skipped?

Evidence to retain: exact source/version, proposed copy, input asset hashes,
owner dispositions, print proofs and pause/resume findings. Owner approval of
this brief alone would not approve final copy, the visual identity or output.
